**🧑 You:** How do I know when is better model to use

**🤖 Claude:** Quick rule of thumb:

**Haiku** — fast, cheap, simple tasks. Good for: NOC alerts, quick summaries, short automation scripts.

**Sonnet** — smarter, better reasoning. Good for: complex code, debugging, long docs, RAG pipelines.

**Opus** — most powerful, slowest, most expensive. Good for: deep analysis, hard architectural decisions.

For your work: Haiku for Telegram bot replies and quick NOC checks, Sonnet for building agents and API course exercises.
