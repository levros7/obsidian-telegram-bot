# Understanding delegation, description, and discernment
**Date:** 2026-03-10

---

**🧑 You:** Delegation, Description, Discernment explain like I am have a problem to understand

**🤖 Claude:** These three words all start with "D" and are about **making decisions about who does what and why**.

---

**Delegation** = *Giving your work to someone else*
You're a manager. You have 10 tasks. You give 7 of them to your team. That's delegation.
> "I trust you to handle this."

---

**Description** = *Explaining what something is*
You write down exactly what a task looks like, what it needs, what the result should be.
> "Here's what this job means, step by step."

---

**Discernment** = *Knowing the difference between two things — especially when it's not obvious*
You look at two similar situations and decide which one is urgent and which one can wait.
> "I can tell these look the same, but they're not."

---

**Together in one sentence:**
> You use **discernment** to decide what to delegate, then write a clear **description** so the other person understands it.
