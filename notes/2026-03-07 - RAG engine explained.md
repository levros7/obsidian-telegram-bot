**🤖 Claude:** ## RAG Engine (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

**The core idea:** Instead of relying only on what an AI *memorized* during training, RAG lets it **look things up first**, then answer.

---

### The 3 Steps

1. **Retrieve** — Search a knowledge base for relevant documents/chunks
2. **Augment** — Add those documents to the AI's prompt as context
3. **Generate** — AI answers using *both* its training + the retrieved info

---

### Simple Analogy
Like an **open-book exam** vs closed-book.
- Without RAG = closed book (only what AI memorized)
- With RAG = open book (AI can check your specific docs first)

---

### Why It Matters
| Problem | RAG Fixes It |
|---|---|
| AI has outdated info | Retrieves current docs |
| AI hallucinates facts | Grounds answers in real sources |
| AI doesn't know *your* data | Searches *your* knowledge base |

---

### Common Use Case
> "Chat with your own documents" tools — upload PDFs, ask questions, AI retrieves the right pages and answers from them.
