# Как поднять ручку тележки
**Date:** 2026-03-30

---

**🧑 You:** Как поднять ручку

**🤖 Claude:** This is a **folding utility wagon/cart** in its collapsed/folded state.

To raise the handle:
1. **Lift the T-shaped handle** upward — it's on a pivot joint
2. It should **click or lock** into the upright position
3. If it feels stuck, check if there's a **small release button/tab** on the joint where the handle meets the frame — press it while lifting

The handle folds flat for storage and pops up for use. No tools needed.

**🧑 You:** Что значит pivot point

**🤖 Claude:** **Pivot point** = точка вращения / шарнир

It's the **joint/hinge** where two parts connect and rotate around each other.

In your cart — it's the **metal joint** where the handle attaches to the frame. That's where it rotates up and down.

**🧑 You:** Что значит upward

**🤖 Claude:** **Upward** = вверх

**🧑 You:** Может нарисуешь а то не понятна

**🤖 Claude:** I can see the joint clearly now. That **ribbed/serrated tab** in the middle is the release button.

**Steps:**
1. **Press/squeeze that ribbed tab** toward the handle
2. **While holding it** — push the handle upward (вверх)
3. It should rotate up and lock in place

Try that!

**🧑 You:** ribbed tab ?

**🤖 Claude:** **Ribbed tab** = рычажок с насечками (тот чёрный зубчатый выступ посередине на фото)
