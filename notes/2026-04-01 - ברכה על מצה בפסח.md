At the Passover Seder, instead of bread you say **"Ha Motzi"** is replaced by **"Al Achilat Matza"** (על אכילת מצה).

The blessing over matzah is two-part:

1. **המוציא** — the regular bread blessing (still said, because matzah is bread-like)
2. **על אכילת מצה** — the specific Passover blessing for the mitzva of eating matza
