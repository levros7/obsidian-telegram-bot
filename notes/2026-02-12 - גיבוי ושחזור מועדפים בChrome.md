**To backup Chrome favorites:**

1. **Built-in sync** (easiest):
   - Sign in to Google Account in Chrome
   - Settings → Sync and Google services
   - Enable "Bookmarks"
   - They auto-backup to your Google Account

2. **Manual export:**
   - Settings → Bookmarks → Bookmark Manager
   - Top right menu (⋮) → Export bookmarks
   - Saves as HTML file

3. **Restore on new device:**
   - Sign into same Google Account
   - Sync restores automatically
