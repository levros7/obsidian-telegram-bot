**🤖 Claude:** **Stock options in simple terms:**

The company gives you the right to buy shares at today's price (called the **strike price**). If the company's value goes up later, you can buy at the old cheap price and sell at the new higher price — the difference is your profit.

**Example:**
- Today the company says a share costs 10 ₪
- You get options worth 7,080 ₪ = 708 shares
- In 3 years the share is worth 30 ₪
- You buy at 10, sell at 30 → profit of 20 ₪ × 708 = **14,160 ₪**

**Vesting** = you don't get all the options at once. A typical schedule is 4 years:
- After year 1: 25% unlocks
- Then a bit more each month/quarter
- After 4 years: 100% is yours

**Key things to know:**
- Options are worth **nothing** if the company doesn't grow or go public/get acquired
- If you leave the company before vesting, you usually lose the unvested portion
- There are **tax implications** when you exercise (use) the options — in Israel this is usually taxed under Section 102

**Bottom line:** Bonus = guaranteed cash now. Options = a bet on the company's future that could pay off big or be worth zero.
