**No — "do Israel has" is incorrect.**

The correct forms are:

- ✅ **"Does Israel have"** — correct question form
- ❌ "Do Israel has" — two errors here:

**Why:**
- With "Israel" (third person singular = he/she/it), you use **"does"** not "do"
- When you use "does," the main verb goes back to base form: **"have"** not "has" (because "does" already carries the grammar weight)

**Easy rule:**
> I/you/we/they → **do + have**
> he/she/it/Israel → **does + have**

So your question should have been: *"Does Israel have its own LLM?"*

This is a very common mistake, especially coming from Hebrew where there's no equivalent to do/does splitting. בעברית אין הבחנה כזו בין do ל-does, אז זה טבעי שזה מבלבל.
