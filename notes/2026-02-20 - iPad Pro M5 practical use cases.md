**🧑 You:** Ps remotely

**🤖 Claude:** **PS Remote Play on iPad**

**Setup:**
1. Download **PS Remote Play** app (free, App Store)
2. On PS5: Settings → System → Remote Play → **Enable**
3. Also enable **Stay Connected to Internet** and **Enable Turning On PS5 from Network**
4. Open app on iPad, sign in with same PSN account, connect

**Tips:**
- Works over WiFi and internet (away from home too)
- Use a **PS5 controller** — connect via Bluetooth to iPad for full experience
- Without controller, on-screen buttons work but feel awkward for action games

**Best for:**
- Slower games (RPGs, strategy, visual novels)
- Fast games work fine on good WiFi, but shooters may feel laggy

**Minimum recommended:** 5 Mbps, but 15+ Mbps is smoother

Want tips on reducing lag?

**🧑 You:** Yes

**🤖 Claude:** **Reducing PS Remote Play Lag**

**On your network:**
- PS5 on **ethernet cable** (not WiFi) — biggest improvement
- iPad on **5GHz WiFi**, not 2.4GHz
- Play close to your router

**In the app settings:**
- Lower resolution to **720p** — much less lag than 1080p
- Lower frame rate to **Standard** if still laggy

**PS5 settings:**
- Settings → System → Remote Play → **Video Quality** → set to 720p/Standard

**Quick test:**
Try 720p first. If smooth, bump to 1080p and see if it holds.

The ethernet cable alone usually cuts lag by half. That's the first thing to fix.
