# Checking Claude usage limits
**Date:** 2026-02-09

---

**🧑 You:** Do I have reached my limits in Claude already?

**🤖 Claude:** Let me check your current usage.
```
This block is not supported on your current device yet.
```

No, you haven't reached any limits with Claude.

**Grammar fix:** "Have I already reached my limits" — "do I have" isn't correct here. We use "have" + past participle for present perfect tense.

Claude.ai has:
- **No message limits** for free or paid users (unlike some AI services)
- **Rate limits** exist (you can't send 1000 messages per second), but they're very high for normal use
- **Token limits** per conversation (context window), but we can start new chats anytime

What made you think you hit a limit?

**🧑 You:** I have so something on Claude code

**🤖 Claude:** **Grammar fixes:**
- "I have so something" → "I have something" or "I want to do something"
- Capital "C" in "Claude Code"

Why: "so" doesn't fit here grammatically. When we list verbs, we need the right form.

- **Show** (display something)?
- **So** (continuing from before)?
- Something else?

Send me the full sentence and I'll help.

**🧑 You:** See in past

**🤖 Claude:** **Grammar fix:** "See in past" → "See in the past" or "Look at past conversations"

Why: We need "the" before time periods in English.
