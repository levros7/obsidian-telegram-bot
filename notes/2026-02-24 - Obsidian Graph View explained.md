**🧑 You:** What is the circular?

**🤖 Claude:** That's the **Obsidian Graph View** — it shows all your notes as dots (nodes) connected by links.

Each dot = one note. Lines between them = connections/links between notes.

The video is about using **LLM + Gemini + Obsidian** as a personal knowledge system — someone spent 1460 hours building it.

**🧑 You:** Is obsidian good for me

**🤖 Claude:** Yes, actually — Obsidian fits ADHD + dysgraphia pretty well:

**Good for you:**
- Visual graph helps see connections (ADHD brain likes this)
- You write in simple Markdown, no complex formatting
- Works offline, your data stays local
- Can connect to AI (like the video shows — LLM + Gemini)

**Potential issues:**
- Setup takes time and can become a distraction ("productivity procrastination")
- Too many plugins = overwhelm

**My honest take:** Start with just basic notes + the graph view. Don't over-configure it early.
