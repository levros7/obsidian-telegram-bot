**Why:** You're mixing Russian and English. "Как парень" = "How a guy" (Russian structure), but you need English word order: subject + verb + object.

**Answer:**
"Guy" = informal word for a man/boy. 

Examples:
- "That guy is cool" 
- "Hey guys!" (greeting for groups)
- "He's a good guy"

**More formal:** man, dude, fellow, lad (British)

**Why:** Russian word order doesn't work in English. You need the article "a" before the noun.

- "He's a simple guy" = straightforward, uncomplicated person
